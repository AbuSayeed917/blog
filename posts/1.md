📜 The Linguistic Perfection of Surah Al-Fatiha

One of the greatest miracles of Surah Al-Fatiha is its perfect balance in language and structure. Every verse and word placement follows a mirrored pattern known as chiasmus, a technique found in classical Arabic and poetry.

🔹 Symmetry in Surah Al-Fatiha
Surah Al-Fatiha consists of 7 verses, with a perfectly symmetrical structure:

1️⃣ Verse 1: Bismillah-ir-Rahman-ir-Rahim → Starts with Divine Mercy.
2️⃣ Verse 2: Praising Allah’s Sovereignty (Lord of the Worlds).
3️⃣ Verse 3: Emphasizing Allah’s Mercy (Ar-Rahman, Ar-Rahim).
4️⃣ Verse 4: The Central Theme – Judgment Day & Worship.
5️⃣ Verse 5: Acknowledging Dependence on Allah (Seeking Help).
6️⃣ Verse 6: Asking for Guidance (The Straight Path).
7️⃣ Verse 7: Distinguishing the Right & Wrong Paths.

💡 Central Message:
The middle verse "You alone we worship, and You alone we ask for help" (إِيَّاكَ نَعْبُدُ وَإِيَّاكَ نَسْتَعِينُ) is the core of the Surah. It transitions from praising Allah to directly addressing Him, making it a unique form of speech in the Quran.
